Snape's Stores Breached on Eve of Final

BY RITA SKEETER, SENIOR CORRESPONDENT

A vial of Felix Felicis has vanished from Snape's private stores — sometime after eleven o'clock, hours before the Quidditch final.

The cabinet was opened with a simple charm. Not forced. Someone who knew the room.

No arrests yet. This reporter is just getting started.

Thistlewood Taken In for Questioning

BY RITA SKEETER, SENIOR CORRESPONDENT

Marigold Thistlewood was taken in before dawn. An empty vial was found in her trunk.

Ingredients withdrawn under a reason that never held up. A corridor left unwatched from 10:47 that night. A letter, half in code, confessing a need for luck that had nothing to do with a House Cup. And a torn corner that fit exactly.

The presses, at last, can rest.
