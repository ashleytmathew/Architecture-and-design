DAILY PROPHET — AUDIO
=====================

Drop four files into this folder with these EXACT names. The deck looks for
them on load and silently does nothing if they're missing, so the deck still
works with no audio at all.

  ambience.mp3   Looping bed. Storm, distant thunder, a crackling fire,
                 scratching quills. Plays under everything once you switch
                 audio on. Pick something with no melody and no obvious loop
                 point — 2 minutes or longer.

  press.mp3      One-shot. Printing press starting up / newspaper clatter.
                 Fires on the STOP PRESS title slide. ~2-3 seconds.

  owl.mp3        One-shot. Owl screech or wingbeats. Fires on each of the
                 five exhibit-cue slides. ~1-2 seconds.

  chime.mp3      One-shot. A bell, gong or clock strike. Fires the moment a
                 countdown hits zero. ~2-3 seconds.


WHERE TO GET THEM (all free, all cleared for commercial/internal use)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Pixabay Audio          pixabay.com/sound-effects
                         No attribution required. Search: "thunder ambience",
                         "printing press", "owl screech", "bell toll".
                         Easiest option — start here.

  YouTube Audio Library  studio.youtube.com > Audio Library
                         Free, no attribution on most tracks. Filter by
                         "Sound effects" > "Ambience" / "Horror".

  Freesound              freesound.org
                         Best quality and biggest library, but CHECK THE
                         LICENCE on each file — some require attribution.
                         Filter to "Creative Commons 0" to avoid the issue.

Do not use the Harry Potter film score. It is not licensed for this.


PLAYING IT INTO MICROSOFT TEAMS
--------------------------------

Attendees hear nothing unless you share system audio.

  1. In Teams, click Share.
  2. Tick "Include computer sound" BEFORE you pick the window.
     (On some builds it's a speaker icon in the share tray.)
  3. Share the browser window — not just a tab, and not "Window" if you
     want reliable audio; full Screen share is the safest.
  4. Test it with one person before the session. Every Teams build handles
     this slightly differently.

Then press A in the deck (or click the speaker pill, bottom left) to start
the ambience. Press A again to mute everything.
